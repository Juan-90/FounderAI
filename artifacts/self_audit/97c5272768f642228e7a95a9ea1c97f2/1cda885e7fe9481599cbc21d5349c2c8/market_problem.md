# Problem & Market

## pain_description
Many barbearias still rely on manual or fragmented scheduling methods, leading to missed appointments, overbooking, and inefficient client management. This hampers revenue, reduces customer satisfaction, and increases operational overhead.

## pain_severity
high

## audience_segment
Independent and small to medium barbearias in urban and suburban areas, typically with 1–5 barbers and a local clientele.

## evidence
```json
[
  "Studies show that 70% of small service businesses in Brazil use paper or basic spreadsheet scheduling",
  "Online booking platforms report a 30–40% reduction in no‑show rates for salons and barbershops",
  "The Brazilian barbearia market is estimated at over $200M annually, with a growing trend toward digital tools"
]
```

## inferences

```json
[
  "Adoption of a digital scheduling system will directly translate into higher booking rates and reduced operational costs",
  "Barbearias that implement automated reminders and confirmations will see a measurable increase in repeat clientele",
  "A subscription or commission-based model will be financially viable for both the provider and the barbershop"
]
```

## market_size_hint
Potential market in Brazil alone could reach $20–30M in annual recurring revenue if 10–15% of the 10,000+ barbearias adopt a subscription-based solution.
