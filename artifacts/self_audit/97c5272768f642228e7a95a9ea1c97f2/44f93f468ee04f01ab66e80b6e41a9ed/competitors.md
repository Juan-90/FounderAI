## status_quo
Small and medium enterprises currently depend on fragmented, manual processes—spreadsheets, ad‑hoc reports, or expensive consulting—to track and manage their carbon emissions. These methods are time‑consuming, prone to errors, and often fail to meet evolving regulatory or market expectations for sustainability transparency.
