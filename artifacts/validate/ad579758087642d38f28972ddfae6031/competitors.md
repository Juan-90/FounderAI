## status_quo
Small businesses currently rely on manual spreadsheets, generic energy dashboards, or external consultants to track and reduce carbon emissions. These methods are time-consuming, lack real-time data integration, and provide limited actionable insights, leading to suboptimal compliance and missed cost-saving opportunities.
